## Context

What prompted the decision. The problem, the constraints that matter,
and anything the reader needs to judge whether the decision still holds.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated in one or two sentences. Specific enough that a
reviewer can disagree.

## Alternatives considered

Each option with a one-line reason it was rejected. Include "do nothing"
when that was a real option.

- **Option A** — rejected because …
- **Option B** — rejected because …

## Consequences

What this closes off (good), what it costs (fair), and what it still
leaves open (honest). This section is for future readers who will revisit
the decision; it is more important than the decision itself.
